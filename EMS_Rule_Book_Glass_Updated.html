01

Department Cooperation & Professional Conduct

🤝 Work Together

All departments are expected to work together and maintain strong cooperation at all times. Teamwork and mutual respect are essential to maintaining an effective and professional environment.

🚫 No Personal Ego or Rivalry

Do not allow personal ego, pride, disagreements, or departmental rivalry to interfere with teamwork or operations.

🗣️ Handle Concerns Professionally

If you have any concerns regarding another department, a fellow member, or any departmental matter, do not create unnecessary conflict or arguments.

📢 Use the Proper Chain of Command

Instead, communicate the issue calmly and professionally with your Duty Lead Officer or Chief.

🧭 Resolve Issues Through Command

Any concerns, misunderstandings, or disagreements should be handled through the appropriate chain of command.

🫱🏻‍🫲🏽 Respect Every Department

Always maintain respect, professionalism, and cooperation when interacting with other departments.

🤝 One community, one team. Departments may have different responsibilities, but we should always work together toward the same goal.

02

General EMS Rules

01 EMS Vehicle Storage

Never leave an EMS vehicle unattended outside the EMS Garage. Before going off duty or flying out, always return your EMS vehicle to the EMS Garage. EMS vehicles are government property and must be stored properly.

Crash protocol: Inform the EMS General channel immediately if your game crashes while your vehicle is outside.

02 No Illegal Activities

Do not participate in illegal activities while using your EMS character. EMS members are expected to maintain a professional and clean record while representing the department.

03 Criminal Items

Do not use or carry criminal-related items such as zip ties, paper bags, or similar items while on duty. EMS personnel must not involve themselves with equipment associated with criminal roles.

04 EMS Vehicle Modifications

Do not modify EMS vehicles with unauthorized engine sounds, body kits, extreme visual modifications, or unapproved upgrades. Keep EMS vehicles simple, clean, and professional.

05 Stay Updated

Always stay updated with the latest EMS rules, announcements, procedures, and server changes. It is every EMS member's responsibility to remain informed.

03

Radio & Communication

06 EMS Radio

Once you come on duty, the EMS radio is mandatory. Always remain available on the radio so you can communicate with other EMS members. Proper radio communication is essential for teamwork and emergency response.

07 Sharing Calls

When 2 or more EMS members are on duty, always use /ra to share calls with your teammates. Do not take every call for yourself when other EMS members are available. Give every EMS member an opportunity to respond.

04

EMS Helicopter Rules

🚁 Helicopter Usage

The EMS helicopter should only be used when necessary.

  • Use it for calls in remote areas.
  • Use it when a location is inaccessible or extremely difficult to reach by EMS vehicle.
  • Use it when the situation genuinely requires helicopter transportation.
  • Do not use it simply for convenience.
  • Do not land on random building rooftops.
  • Only access the helicopter from the designated helipad.
  • Always operate it responsibly and professionally.
05

Hospital Rules

09 Hospital Props

Do not place any props inside or around the hospital unless approved by the Head of Government. Do not add, remove, or move hospital props without proper authorization.

10 Patient Arrival Procedure

  1. Guide the patient to the Doctor's Office / Doctor Room.
  2. Ask the patient to remain there for examination and treatment.
  3. Do not provide critical treatment in the hospital lobby or hallway.
  4. All serious or critical injuries must be treated inside the designated Treatment Room.
  5. Keep hospital hallways and public areas clear.

11 Patient Treatment & Discharge

Patients must remain at the hospital until their injuries have been properly treated. After treatment, ensure the patient's condition has been fully cleared before allowing them to leave. Do not rush the treatment or discharge process.

12 Treatment Area

Do not treat critical injuries in the EMS lobby, hallway, or other public areas. Use the appropriate Treatment Room for serious injuries and keep the hospital environment organized and professional.

06

End-of-Shift Responsibilities

Before going off duty, every EMS member is responsible for leaving the department ready for the next crew.

Return all EMS equipment to its proper storage location.
Return any medical equipment you have used.
Return your EMS vehicle to the EMS Garage.
Refuel the EMS vehicle if necessary.
Repair any damage before storing the vehicle.
Dispose of used medical supplies appropriately.
Report damaged or missing equipment to command staff.
Ensure the vehicle and equipment are ready for the next EMS member.

The next EMS member should never have to repair a vehicle, search for equipment, or clean up after the previous shift.

07

Uniform & Professionalism

13 Uniform Standards

Professional appearance is expected at all times while on duty. Your uniform represents the EMS Department and the entire medical team.

Uniform must be clean • complete • appropriate • suitable for the current duty assignment

14 Professional Conduct

Always maintain professional behavior while representing EMS. Treat patients, civilians, and fellow EMS members with respect. Do not abuse EMS privileges or use your EMS position for personal benefit. Follow instructions from EMS command staff.
